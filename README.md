## About

You are a man pushed to the edge.  
Personal problems force you to seek your last chance at success — gambling.

But how far are you willing to go?

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/whyhvru/gamejam-gambler-2024/blob/main/screenshot-2.png?raw=true" width="80%">
</p>

## Features

- Narrative-driven simulator
- Two gambling mini-games
- Resource and risk management
- Limited daytime system
- Debt system with loans and interest
- In-game chat and messaging window

## Game Jam

Created for the 2-week [Pixel Jam](https://itch.io/jam/pixel-gamejam-1).

Theme:
> **Expectation vs Reality**

The game was developed in 7 days and placed **6th** in the jam.

We faced several difficulties during development that delayed production early on, but despite all the challenges, we did our best to finish the project in time.

We hope you enjoy the result.
